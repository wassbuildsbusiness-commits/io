Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what personal information FewWantLeads ("FWL", "we", "us") collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, business details and billing information, for example when you book a call, sign an agreement or pay an invoice.
  • Booking information: when you book through our calendar (Calendly), we receive the details and answers you enter there.
  • Account access and content: footage, scripts and the account access you choose to share so we can deliver our services.

Device and usage data

When you visit our website we may automatically collect your IP address, browser and device type, operating system and the pages you visit.

Cookies and tracking technologies

We and our embedded tools (such as Google Fonts, Calendly and video hosting) may use cookies to make the site work, remember preferences and understand traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

How we use your information

  • To deliver, manage and improve our services
  • To communicate with you about calls, projects and invoices
  • To keep our website and systems secure and prevent fraud

We don't use your information for purposes unrelated to these.

Service providers

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with providers that help us run the business, such as hosting, scheduling, payments, file storage and analytics, and only as needed for them to do that work.

Legal obligations

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

Business transfers

If FWL is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, subject to legal or contractual obligations, and you can opt out of non-essential communications at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR.

Third-party websites

Our site links to other websites and platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

Our services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
