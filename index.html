FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I actually have to do?

Record for 2 hours a week. That's it. We send you scripts ready to read, you record in one session, and we handle strategy, editing, thumbnails, descriptions, uploads and every short.

What is FWL?

FewWantLeads (FWL) is a social media agency for experts with high-ticket offers. We script, edit, package and publish your long-form and short-form content on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X — built to bring you qualified sales calls, not just views.

What is your turnaround time?

Your first short goes live within 72 hours of kickoff and your first long-form video within 14 days. After that, edited videos are delivered on a fixed weekly schedule.

How will we communicate?

You get a shared channel with our team for day-to-day questions, a shared folder for footage and scripts, and a weekly report every Friday. Strategy calls happen monthly, or whenever you need one.

What time zone are you in?

We work on Central European Time (CET) and overlap with US mornings, so most clients get same-day replies.

What editing styles do you work in?

Retention-focused talking-head, documentary and storytelling, educational explainers with b-roll and motion graphics, and podcast-to-shorts clipping. We match the style to your niche and your audience.

Who is this for?

Coaches, consultants, agency owners, finance educators and B2B founders whose offer is worth $2,000+ per client. One extra sale a month usually pays for the whole plan.

How fast will I see results?

Your first short goes live within 72 hours of kickoff and your first long-form video within 14 days. Booked calls typically start building in the first 30–60 days.

Do I need an existing channel?

No. We set it up and grow it from zero. If you already have one, we optimize what's there.

Why don't you upload to TikTok?

TikTok requires the creator to post from their own account. We deliver edited files and captions ready to post in seconds.

What's the difference between monthly and the 90-day plan?

Same deliverables. The 90-day plan is paid for three months and saves about 11% — and gives the system enough time to compound.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Most clients start on YouTube or All-Platform and add platforms as results come in. Changes apply from the next month.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. 90-day and founding plans run for the full term.
